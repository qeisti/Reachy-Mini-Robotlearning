\section{Introduction}
\color{structure}\textit{Ziel: ca. 0,6 Seiten. Fuenf Absaetze, je 3--5 Saetze. Roter Faden: Zugang zur Robotik $\rightarrow$ Hemmschwelle $\rightarrow$ Design als Hebel $\rightarrow$ was fehlt $\rightarrow$ was diese Arbeit tut.}\color{black}

\todo{Absatz 1 -- Thema.} Robots are moving from industrial cells into everyday
environments, which makes the quality of a first encounter a design problem
rather than a side effect.\needcite{HRI in everyday settings}

\todo{Absatz 2 -- Motivation.} Psychological research shows that appearance and
emotional responsiveness systematically shape how approachable an artefact is
perceived to be: baby-schema features activate reward and caretaking
responses\needcite{Lorenz/Glocker}, cute stimuli lower
vigilance\needcite{[1] Yim}, and responsive behaviour drives anthropomorphic
attribution\needcite{[2] Rosenthal-von der Puetten}.

\todo{Absatz 3 -- Problem.} These effects are usually demonstrated on images,
videos or on complex humanoid platforms.\needcite{[3] Rogel -- naechstliegende
Vorarbeit} It is not obvious how far they carry over to a robot whose entire
expressive channel consists of head pose and two antennas, and which must
react in real time on commodity hardware.

\todo{Absatz 4 -- Beitrag (den Satz scharf formulieren!).} This paper presents a
behaviour architecture for the Reachy Mini that deliberately targets the
mechanisms above. It is built entirely from commodity perception components --
Google MediaPipe for face and gesture input and Vosk for offline speech
recognition -- so that the contribution lies in the behaviour layer rather than
in perception. Four skill configurations of that architecture are evaluated
with respect to reaction time and repeatability.

\todo{Absatz 5 -- Aufbau.} Section II reviews the relevant background, Section
III describes platform, implementation and evaluation design, Section IV
reports the measurements, and Section V concludes.

\todo{Zuschreibung von Absichten an bewegte Objekte; fMRI-Befunde zu Empathie

\todo{Bindungs-/Intimitaetsmodelle: Naehe entsteht, wenn das Gegenueber zeitnah

Verhalten gelesen.}\needcite{[7] Reis \& Shaver}

\subsection{Real-Time Perception Toolkits}
\todo{Zwei bis drei Saetze: Warum heute nicht mehr selbst trainiert wird.}
Modern interactive systems typically rely on pre-trained, CPU-capable
pipelines rather than on task-specific training. Google
MediaPipe~\cite{Lugaresi2019MediaPipe} provides such pipelines for face mesh,
hand and gesture estimation, and Vosk~\cite{Vosk} offers offline speech
recognition for a range of languages. Both run locally, which keeps latency
low and avoids any network dependency -- a direct prerequisite for the
contingency requirement discussed in Section II-D.

\subsection{Expressive Robots with Reduced Degrees of Freedom}
\todo{Spannweite von anthropomorphen Koepfen mit vielen Gesichtsaktoren bis zu
Systemen, die Affekt nur ueber Kopfbewegung und Antennen vermitteln. Fuer den
Reachy Mini selbst: Beobachter bewerten positiven Gefuehlsausdruck als deutlich
waermer, freundlicher und vertrauenswuerdiger -- damit ist der reduzierte Kanal
grundsaetzlich ausreichend. Ggf. Keepon und Cozmo ergaenzen.}\needcite{[3] Rogel, [5] Luetkebohle, minimal social robots}

\subsection{Platform}
\todo{Reachy Mini: 6-DoF-Kopf (Stewart-Plattform), Body-Yaw, zwei Antennen,

\subsection{Perception}
All perception is performed on-device. Facial input is provided by the
\textit{FaceLandmarker} task of Google MediaPipe~\cite{Lugaresi2019MediaPipe},
which returns 478 facial landmarks together with 52 blendshape coefficients
per frame; the coefficients are aggregated into four affective categories by
threshold. Hand input uses the MediaPipe \textit{GestureRecognizer} task for
static poses, complemented by a custom waving detector. Speech is transcribed
offline by Vosk~\cite{Vosk} with the small German model.

\todo{Kernidee erklaeren und als eigene Abbildung zeichnen: Ein Rechteck aus

separate Face-Detection-Stufe und spart eine Inferenz pro Frame.}

which communicates with a daemon process that drives either the physical robot
or a MuJoCo~\cite{Todorov2012MuJoCo} simulation. \todo{Versionen ergaenzen und
